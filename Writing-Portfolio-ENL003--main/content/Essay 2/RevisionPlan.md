# Revision Plan for Essay #2

For my deep paragraph revision, I want to revise the paragraph where I discuss the Oankali's forced genetic trade because this is where my argument about power, consent, and bio-power can become much clearer.

### Before:

"Although the Oankali claim to cure humanity's destructive hierarchy, their forced genetic trade risks reproducing the same logic of domination that they condemn. The Oankali survive by exchanging genetic material with other species (Butler). They understand this practice as natural, necessary, and even generous. From their perspective, humans will not simply disappear. They will continue in altered form through hybrid children. However, from the human perspective, this 'trade' is not truly voluntary. The survivors do not receive a meaningful choice about whether humanity should remain biologically human. The Oankali decide that human beings cannot be trusted with their own future, and then they impose a solution."

### After:

Although the Oankali describe their genetic trade as a rescue, Butler shows that rescue becomes dangerous when one group has the power to decide what another group's future should be. The Oankali claim they are saving humans from their "fatal" combination of intelligence and hierarchy, but they also control human bodies, reproduction, and survival (Butler). This is where bio-power becomes useful to my argument: the Oankali do not only rule humans through force; they rule by managing life itself. Their good intentions make them blind to the harm they cause because they see consent as less important than biological improvement. Like Homelander in *The Boys*, who believes people should thank him because they "need" him, the Oankali turn dependence into justification. Butler therefore suggests that unequal power can make even a savior reproduce domination while believing it is acting for the greater good.

I changed this paragraph by making the topic sentence more argumentative and by connecting the evidence more directly to my main claim. The original paragraph explained the Oankali's actions, but it did not fully analyze why their version of rescue is harmful. The revised paragraph adds bio-power as a theory and uses Homelander as a comparison, so the paragraph now shows how control can hide behind protection. I still need to improve the transition between Bookchin's social ecology and bio-power so the essay does not feel like it is using too many separate theories.

My first priority for the final draft is to narrow the thesis so it focuses on how unequal power makes powerful figures blind to the harm they cause through "good" intentions. My third priority is to revise each topic sentence so it introduces an argument, not just a topic.
